  <h2 style="font:var(--text-heading-m);margin-bottom:28px;">Currently incubating</h2>

  <div class="ff-card ff-card--raised" style="margin-bottom:32px;">
    <div class="ff-eyebrow">Food tracking &amp; meal planning</div>
    <div class="ff-card__title">Holo Foods</div>
    <div class="ff-card__body">
      <p style="margin:0 0 16px;">Holo Foods is a food tracking and meal planning app, built UK-first and mobile-first. Log what you eat, plan meals ahead, and see nutrition roll up by day and week &mdash; with the numbers one tap away and the day's progress visible at a glance.</p>
      <p style="margin:0;">Behind the app is a platform: a verified nutrition catalog and an embeddable tracker that other products, such as telehealth providers, gyms, and insurers, can build on instead of building food tracking themselves. The consumer app is how we prove the capability and grow the dataset.</p>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="process-grid">
    <div class="process-step">
      <div class="ff-card__index">Log</div>
      <div class="ff-card__title">Estimate-friendly tracking</div>
      <div class="ff-card__body">Scan a barcode or snap a label photo to pull real nutrition data, then log an amount by weight, by count, by eye, or in your own units. An approximate entry in seconds beats an exact one that never happens. On Apple Watch, water logs in a single tap and syncs to Apple Health.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="process-step">
      <div class="ff-card__index">Plan</div>
      <div class="ff-card__title">Meal planning and insight</div>
      <div class="ff-card__body">Plan meals ahead and track progress against daily targets. A shopping companion reads a scanned product or a shelf photo, flags allergens and ultra-processed foods, and suggests better options that fit your goals and the way you eat.</div>
    </div>
    <div class="process-step">
      <div class="ff-card__index">Trust</div>
      <div class="ff-card__title">Verified nutrition data</div>
      <div class="ff-card__body">Built on Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central, with a verification layer on top: user corrections are checked against photo evidence, and every value carries its source. Health diary data stays private, including from our own staff.</div>
    </div>
  </div>
